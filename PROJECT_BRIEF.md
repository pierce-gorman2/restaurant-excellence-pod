# PROJECT BRIEF FOR CODEX

## Project name
Restaurant Excellence

## Current scope only
Right now, this website should ONLY function as a podcast hosting platform for three Dave's Hot Chicken markets:

1. DHC of Kansas
2. DHC of San Antonio
3. DHC of Nashville

Do not build it yet as a consulting business, article website, or full training portal. Structure it so those can be added later.

## Goal
Create a premium, modern, leadership-oriented website that feels like an internal media platform for operators and leaders. It should be clean, slightly bold, and professional.

## Brand direction
- Sophisticated, sharp, premium
- Restaurant and operations focused
- Strong leadership feel
- Modern but not flashy
- Designed to scale later

## Core pages to eventually support
For now, the homepage is enough, but the architecture should make room for:
- Homepage
- Market pages
- Individual episode pages
- Transcript pages
- Resource pages later

## Core functionality for this stage
- A homepage with a strong hero section
- A section for the three markets
- A podcast episode feed
- Filtering by market
- Episode cards with title, market, category, duration, and listening links
- Placeholder links for Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube

## Content categories
Likely podcast categories over time:
- Leadership
- Operations
- Culture
- Store Spotlights
- Interviews
- Training

## Design notes
- Dark or neutral premium theme is good
- Use strong typography
- Clean spacing
- Rounded cards are okay
- Make it feel like a real brand, not a template
- It should feel relevant to restaurant operators and franchise leadership

## Important constraints
- Keep the site narrow in scope for now
- This is NOT the full Restaurant Excellence platform yet
- Do not overbuild unnecessary sections
- Do not add random generic agency language
- Keep the copy tight and purposeful

## Later expansion ideas
These are for later, not now:
- Searchable transcripts
- Team login/private access
- Course and training modules
- Articles and book breakdowns
- Store-level pages
- Leadership resources
- Newsletter signup
- CMS integration

## Technical preference
If you rebuild this from the starter, keep the codebase clean and organized so it can later move into a modern framework like Next.js.
